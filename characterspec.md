**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Define the appearance, personality, backstory, abilities, weaknesses, and gameplay identity of four new characters.

# 1. Bobbie Macalanger

## Basic Information

- **Full Name:** Bobbie Macalanger
- **Alias:** The Street Champ
- **Species:** Human
- **Role:** Boxer / Close-Combat Fighter
- **Personality:** Confident, energetic, competitive, loyal
- **Combat Style:** Heavy punches, quick footwork, and powerful combos
- **Difficulty:** Easy to learn, difficult to master

## Backstory

Bobbie Macalanger grew up in a busy neighborhood where everyone knew everyone, and respect had to be earned rather than given. As a kid, Bobbie was energetic, stubborn, and always ready to defend his friends. Unfortunately, his temper often got him into trouble.

Everything changed when his grandfather introduced him to boxing. At first, Bobbie only wanted to prove that he was the toughest guy around. However, after years of training at a small neighborhood gym, he learned that real strength came from discipline, patience, and knowing when not to throw a punch.

Bobbie quickly became one of the gym's most promising fighters. His explosive power and unpredictable movement made him difficult to defeat. Despite his success, he refused to leave his neighborhood behind and continued helping younger kids discover boxing.

One night, a mysterious tournament organizer invited Bobbie to compete in a series of underground challenges. The prize was more than money: the winner would gain access to a secret organization searching for powerful fighters to protect the city.

Bobbie accepted immediately.

He believes he can beat anyone in the ring, but his greatest challenge might be learning that not every problem can be solved with his fists.

## Appearance

- **Hair:** A large, rounded black afro that serves as his signature feature.
- **Face:** Strong jawline, expressive eyebrows, and a confident grin.
- **Build:** Athletic, muscular upper body, powerful shoulders, and strong legs.
- **Clothing:** Sleeveless boxing hoodie or athletic tank top, boxing shorts, and hand wraps.
- **Footwear:** Bright, high-top sneakers with thick soles and distinctive colored accents.
- **Accessories:** Sports wristbands, a gold chain, and a small championship medal hanging from his gym bag.
- **Color Palette:** Red, black, white, and gold.
- **Visual Silhouette:** Large afro, broad shoulders, oversized sneakers, and raised boxing gloves.

## Personality

Bobbie is loud, funny, and full of confidence. He enjoys friendly competition and often gives opponents nicknames before a fight.

He is extremely loyal to his friends and has a strong sense of fairness. Although he sometimes acts before thinking, he never abandons someone who needs help.

**Catchphrase:** "Step into my ring, and let's see what you've got!"

## Abilities

### 1. Macalanger Punch
A powerful straight punch that deals high damage to a single opponent.

### 2. Sneaker Dash
Bobbie rapidly dashes forward using his athletic footwork, allowing him to dodge attacks or close the distance.

### 3. Three-Piece Combo
A fast combination of three punches. The final punch knocks the opponent backward.

### 4. Ultimate — Championship Uppercut
Bobbie charges his fist and delivers a devastating uppercut that launches enemies into the air.

## Strengths

- High close-range damage.
- Strong against individual opponents.
- Good mobility for a heavy fighter.
- Easy-to-understand combat mechanics.

## Weaknesses

- Limited long-range attacks.
- Can struggle against enemies that constantly keep their distance.
- Some powerful attacks leave him vulnerable if they miss.

## Gameplay Identity

Bobbie is the ideal character for players who enjoy aggressive combat and satisfying melee attacks. His gameplay rewards timing, positioning, and maintaining pressure on opponents.

# 2. R.O.C.K.Y

## Basic Information

- **Full Name:** R.O.C.K.Y
- **Meaning:** Robotic Offensive Combat Kinetic Yard-unit
- **Alias:** The Iron Bull
- **Species:** Experimental Combat Robot
- **Role:** Tank / Heavy Defender
- **Personality:** Literal-minded, stubborn, protective, unintentionally funny
- **Combat Style:** Heavy impacts, charges, defensive abilities, and crowd control
- **Difficulty:** Medium

## Backstory

R.O.C.K.Y was originally designed in a secret industrial laboratory as an experimental security robot. His creators wanted to build a machine capable of protecting important facilities from dangerous intruders.

The project was considered a failure because R.O.C.K.Y refused to follow certain orders.

Whenever his programming identified an innocent person in danger, he would ignore his assigned mission and protect them instead. The engineers repeatedly attempted to correct this behavior, but the robot's mysterious decision-making system continued to develop in unexpected ways.

His most recognizable feature was added during an experimental upgrade: a pair of reinforced mechanical bull horns designed to improve his ability to break through obstacles.

The upgrade worked a little too well.

During a final stress test, R.O.C.K.Y charged through three reinforced walls, destroyed the testing equipment, and accidentally escaped the laboratory.

Outside, he encountered a group of street performers who mistook him for a giant robotic mascot. They gave him the nickname R.O.C.K.Y, and he decided to keep it because it sounded, in his own words, "structurally impressive."

Over time, R.O.C.K.Y began to understand the world beyond his programming. He discovered music, friendship, jokes, and the strange human habit of giving machines names.

Now he travels from place to place, searching for the truth about his creators while protecting anyone who cannot protect themselves.

His greatest dream is to understand what it means to have a heart, even though he technically has a very expensive power core instead.

## Appearance

- **Head:** A compact mechanical head with two large, curved bull horns.
- **Eyes:** Glowing blue optical sensors that change color when his systems detect danger.
- **Body:** Massive armored torso with a broad chest, thick mechanical arms, and reinforced legs.
- **Materials:** Scratched steel, dark gunmetal, and heavy industrial plating.
- **Horns:** Oversized metal horns with illuminated energy lines running through them.
- **Chest:** A circular power core visible through a protective transparent panel.
- **Arms:** One reinforced fist and one mechanical hand with interchangeable tools.
- **Legs:** Heavy hydraulic legs with shock-absorbing feet.
- **Accessories:** Warning labels, a small painted smile on one shoulder, and an old laboratory identification tag.
- **Color Palette:** Gunmetal gray, black, electric blue, and orange warning details.
- **Visual Silhouette:** Broad shoulders, huge bull horns, a glowing chest core, and heavy mechanical limbs.

## Personality

R.O.C.K.Y is serious about protecting people but has a surprisingly innocent understanding of everyday life.

He takes jokes literally, struggles with sarcasm, and occasionally announces his calculations during conversations.

He is stubborn and rarely changes his mind once he has selected a target. However, his loyalty is almost impossible to break.

He sometimes attempts to imitate human behavior, such as giving a thumbs-up after a successful battle, even when his mechanical hand is holding a large piece of debris.

**Catchphrase:** "THREAT DETECTED. HORNS ACTIVATED."

## Abilities

### 1. Bull Rush
R.O.C.K.Y lowers his horns and charges forward, knocking enemies aside and damaging obstacles.

### 2. Steel Fist
A heavy punch that deals significant damage and can interrupt enemy attacks.

### 3. Fortress Mode
R.O.C.K.Y locks his legs into the ground and activates reinforced armor, reducing incoming damage for a short period.

### 4. Ultimate — Full Charge Overdrive
R.O.C.K.Y's power core overloads safely for a limited time. His horns glow brightly as he performs a devastating charge that sends nearby enemies flying.

## Strengths

- Very high health and durability.
- Excellent at controlling groups of enemies.
- Strong against obstacles and armored opponents.
- Can protect teammates and withstand heavy attacks.

## Weaknesses

- Slow movement compared with lighter characters.
- Large body makes it difficult to dodge narrow attacks.
- Energy-based abilities require cooldowns.
- Vulnerable to enemies that exploit his predictable movement.

## Gameplay Identity

R.O.C.K.Y is the team's walking fortress. He is designed for players who prefer durability, powerful attacks, and controlling the battlefield instead of relying on speed.

# 3. Ninja Main

## Basic Information

- **Real Name:** Unknown
- **Alias:** Ninja Main
- **Species:** Human
- **Role:** Agile Fighter / Beginner Assassin
- **Personality:** Enthusiastic, awkward, determined, imaginative
- **Combat Style:** Quick movement, improvised ninja techniques, smoke bombs, and surprise attacks
- **Difficulty:** Medium to High

## Backstory

Ninja Main has loved ninjas for as long as he can remember.

As a child, he watched old martial arts movies, collected ninja toys, and practiced dramatic poses in front of his bedroom mirror. He spent countless hours studying fictional ninja techniques and memorizing the names of legendary weapons.

Unfortunately, watching ninja movies and actually becoming a ninja turned out to be two very different things.

His first attempt at stealth ended when he tripped over a bucket. His first attempt at climbing a wall resulted in a trip to the local clinic. His first homemade smoke bomb filled his own room with smoke and set off every alarm in the building.

Despite these disasters, he never gave up.

One day, he discovered an abandoned training dojo hidden behind an old marketplace. Inside, he found a mysterious scroll containing a series of exercises designed to test speed, discipline, awareness, and courage.

He decided that this was his destiny.

Ninja Main began training every day. He learned to move quietly, throw practice shuriken, and disappear behind objects. His techniques were still rough, and he often announced his own attacks before performing them, but his determination was extraordinary.

He eventually entered a dangerous competition to prove himself worthy of becoming a true ninja.

He is not a master yet. He is not even particularly stealthy.

But he has something that many experienced warriors have forgotten: the willingness to keep trying after every embarrassing failure.

His goal is simple: become the greatest ninja the world has ever seen.

His first objective is to stop stepping on his own throwing stars.

## Appearance

- **Head:** A black ninja hood with a slightly oversized mask covering the lower half of his face.
- **Eyes:** Large, expressive eyes that reveal his excitement and nervousness.
- **Hair:** A few untidy strands of dark hair occasionally escape from beneath his hood.
- **Build:** Slim, lightweight, and athletic, with room for further physical development.
- **Clothing:** An improvised ninja outfit made from dark fabric, mismatched protective pieces, and a training belt.
- **Footwear:** Lightweight black ninja shoes with flexible soles.
- **Weapons:** Practice shuriken, a wooden training sword, and small smoke bombs.
- **Accessories:** A scroll tied to his belt, a collection of lucky charms, and a tiny notebook filled with ninja techniques.
- **Color Palette:** Black, dark purple, muted red, and silver.
- **Visual Silhouette:** Pointed hood, face mask, loose fabric, and an oversized collection of beginner ninja equipment.

His outfit should look functional but slightly improvised. Unlike a legendary ninja, he has not yet perfected his equipment or movement.

## Personality

Ninja Main takes himself very seriously, even when everyone else can see that he is still learning.

He practices mysterious poses, whispers dramatic statements, and sometimes tries to disappear while standing directly in front of an opponent.

He is easily embarrassed when his techniques fail, but he recovers quickly and treats every mistake as a lesson.

Beneath his awkward behavior is a genuinely brave person who refuses to abandon his friends.

**Catchphrase:** "You have witnessed only 1% of my ninja potential!"

## Abilities

### 1. Shadow Step
Ninja Main performs a quick dash in a chosen direction. With good timing, he can avoid incoming attacks.

### 2. Rookie Shuriken
He throws a small number of shuriken toward enemies. The projectiles deal moderate damage, but their spread makes precision important.

### 3. Smoke Panic
Ninja Main throws a smoke bomb that briefly obscures the battlefield, giving him an opportunity to reposition or escape.

### 4. Ultimate — Almost Invisible
Ninja Main attempts his most advanced stealth technique. He becomes temporarily difficult to detect, gains increased movement speed, and performs a rapid surprise attack when the effect ends.

A small visual gag can occur when the ability activates: he briefly strikes an unnecessarily dramatic pose before vanishing.

## Strengths

- Very high mobility.
- Effective at dodging attacks.
- Can surprise enemies and reposition quickly.
- Versatile toolkit with both ranged and melee options.

## Weaknesses

- Low health compared with heavier characters.
- Requires precise timing.
- Some attacks are less reliable than those of experienced fighters.
- Can be punished heavily if a dodge or stealth ability is mistimed.

## Gameplay Identity

Ninja Main rewards skill, creativity, and movement. He is designed for players who enjoy fast characters and mastering difficult mechanics.

His progression could include a character-specific development system in which his abilities become more reliable as he completes training challenges.

For example:
- **Beginner:** Unstable shuriken throws and short dashes.
- **Intermediate:** Better accuracy, longer dashes, and improved stealth.
- **Advanced:** Chained dodges, precise projectile attacks, and extended surprise combos.

The intention is to let the player grow alongside the character rather than making him a perfect ninja from the beginning.

# 4. ZOO-mbie

## Basic Information

- **Full Name:** Unknown
- **Alias:** ZOO-mbie
- **Species:** Zombie / Former Human
- **Role:** Unpredictable Bruiser / Area Disruption
- **Personality:** Curious, hungry, clumsy, surprisingly friendly
- **Combat Style:** Unusual movement, infectious-looking slime effects, wild swings, and environmental attacks
- **Difficulty:** Easy to Medium

## Backstory

Before becoming ZOO-mbie, he was an ordinary human with an extraordinary love for wildlife.

He worked as a safari tour guide, taking visitors across vast grasslands to observe lions, elephants, giraffes, and other animals in their natural habitats.

He was patient with tourists, passionate about conservation, and capable of identifying animal tracks from a distance. His only unusual habit was talking to animals as if they could understand every word.

During one expedition, his safari group discovered an abandoned research station near a remote wildlife reserve. The building had once belonged to a scientific team studying a mysterious microorganism found in the surrounding ecosystem.

Curious about the abandoned equipment, he approached the station to investigate.

Inside, a damaged containment unit suddenly released a strange airborne substance. He attempted to escape, but collapsed before reaching the exit.

When he finally regained consciousness, something was terribly wrong.

His skin had turned a sickly green, his movements had become uncoordinated, and his heartbeat had disappeared. He was, by every reasonable definition, a zombie.

At first, he believed his life was over.

However, he soon discovered that he could still think, remember his past, and recognize the animals he had spent years protecting. Even stranger, some animals appeared far less frightened of him than they should have been.

He returned to the safari reserve and began living among its abandoned research vehicles and overgrown observation towers.

Tourists who occasionally spotted him started calling him ZOO-mbie, a name that combined his former profession with his undead condition.

Although he looks terrifying, ZOO-mbie has retained much of his old personality. He still enjoys watching wildlife documentaries, collecting animal photographs, and protecting creatures in danger.

He now searches for the source of the infection, hoping to discover whether his condition can be reversed.

Until then, he has learned to make the most of his unusual new body.

After all, being a zombie has certain advantages when your arm occasionally falls off and you can simply pick it up again.

## Appearance

- **Skin:** Patchy green skin with darker discoloration and visible cracks.
- **Face:** One drooping eyelid, a crooked smile, uneven teeth, and a slightly exposed cheekbone.
- **Eyes:** One cloudy yellow eye and one glowing orange eye.
- **Hair:** Messy, dusty brown hair with small leaves and twigs tangled in it.
- **Build:** Tall, slightly hunched, and lanky, with oversized hands and uneven shoulders.
- **Clothing:** Torn safari shirt, dusty cargo shorts or trousers, and a battered field vest.
- **Footwear:** One worn hiking boot and one damaged outdoor shoe, giving him an uneven walk.
- **Accessories:** A cracked pair of binoculars, a faded safari identification badge, a small animal field guide, and a torn sun hat.
- **Special Details:** Small vines grow through parts of his clothing, and a faint green glow sometimes appears around his hands.
- **Color Palette:** Moss green, dusty brown, faded khaki, and sickly yellow.
- **Visual Silhouette:** Hunched posture, long dangling arms, a crooked safari hat, and an uneven gait.

His design should balance creepy and comedic elements. He should look unmistakably undead without losing the recognizable appearance of a former safari guide.

## Personality

ZOO-mbie is slow to react but surprisingly observant.

He occasionally forgets that he is dead, attempting to perform ordinary human activities such as checking his pulse or complaining about being tired.

He is fascinated by animals and becomes unusually excited whenever he sees one. He may interrupt a battle to stare at a butterfly or attempt to communicate with an enemy that resembles a wildlife species.

Despite his appearance, he is gentle toward innocent creatures and fiercely protective of his friends.

His appetite is unusual: he craves rotten fruit, old vegetation, and occasionally objects that should never be considered food.

**Catchphrase:** "Brains? Nah... got any bananas?"

## Abilities

### 1. Rotten Swing
ZOO-mbie swings his long arms in a wide arc, damaging enemies standing nearby.

### 2. Undead Shuffle
He lurches forward with an unpredictable movement pattern. His awkward gait makes his movement difficult for enemies to anticipate.

### 3. Safari Senses
ZOO-mbie uses his old tracking skills to reveal nearby enemies, hidden paths, or important objects for a short period.

### 4. Ultimate — Zombie Stampede
ZOO-mbie releases a loud, unnatural roar that attracts a swarm of infected wildlife shadows or stylized zombie creatures.

The swarm rushes through the battlefield, disrupting enemies and dealing damage across a wide area.

The ability should feel chaotic and visually distinctive, reinforcing his connection to the safari and the strange infection that transformed him.

## Strengths

- Strong area-of-effect attacks.
- Unpredictable movement.
- Good at disrupting groups of enemies.
- Can reveal hidden enemies or objects through tracking.
- Potentially high survivability if the game includes regeneration mechanics.

## Weaknesses

- Slower and less precise than agile characters.
- Some attacks have wide animations that are easy to anticipate.
- Can struggle against opponents who maintain distance.
- His unusual abilities may require careful positioning to maximize their effectiveness.

## Gameplay Identity

ZOO-mbie is an unconventional bruiser who combines crowd control, exploration utility, and comedic unpredictability.

He is ideal for players who want a character with a memorable visual design and unusual mechanics.

His wildlife background can also support special interactions with environmental objects, hidden collectibles, and animal-themed side quests.

# 5. Character Balance and Design Guidelines

The four characters should have clearly different gameplay identities, ensuring that each one provides a distinct experience.

| Character | Primary Role | Main Strength | Main Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bobbie Macalanger | Boxer | Powerful close-range combos | Limited range |
| R.O.C.K.Y | Tank | Durability and crowd control | Slow movement |
| Ninja Main | Agile fighter | Speed and evasiveness | Low health |
| ZOO-mbie | Bruiser | Area disruption and tracking | Unpredictable attacks |

## Shared Design Principles

- Each character should have a recognizable silhouette, even when viewed from a distance.
- Each character should have a unique movement style that reflects their personality.
- Basic attacks should feel different in speed, weight, animation, and impact.
- Special abilities should have clear visual and audio feedback.
- Ultimate abilities should be visually distinctive and easy to recognize during gameplay.
- Character weaknesses should create interesting gameplay decisions rather than making any character frustrating to use.
- Animations should reinforce character personality, including idle poses, victory animations, damage reactions, and defeat animations.

## Suggested Idle Animations

**Bobbie Macalanger:** Bounces lightly on his feet, shadowboxes, and adjusts his sneakers.

**R.O.C.K.Y:** Scans the environment, releases a burst of steam, and occasionally tests his horns against an imaginary target.

**Ninja Main:** Attempts a dramatic ninja pose, checks whether anyone noticed, and quickly tries again when he realizes his balance is off.

**ZOO-mbie:** Scratches his head, adjusts his safari hat, and briefly attempts to eat a leaf before deciding it is not particularly tasty.

## Suggested Victory Animations

**Bobbie Macalanger:** Raises both boxing gloves, celebrates like a championship winner, and points confidently at the camera.

**R.O.C.K.Y:** Slams his fists together, charges his horns, and proudly announces that the threat has been eliminated.

**Ninja Main:** Performs an elaborate series of ninja hand signs, attempts a backflip, and barely manages to land on his feet.

**ZOO-mbie:** Celebrates with a crooked dance, loses one shoe, picks it up, and continues dancing as if nothing happened.

# 6. Future Expansion Ideas

These ideas are optional and can be implemented as the game develops.

- **Bobbie Macalanger:** Rival boxers, championship tournaments, and customizable sneakers or boxing gloves.
- **R.O.C.K.Y:** Discoverable laboratory records, armor upgrades, and story missions involving his original creators.
- **Ninja Main:** Training challenges, unlockable ninja techniques, and a progression path toward becoming a true ninja master.
- **ZOO-mbie:** Safari exploration missions, wildlife encounters, and a quest to discover the origin of the infection.

Each character should have a personal objective that can connect to the larger game storyline.

Their personalities should also influence dialogue and interactions with other characters. Bobbie may challenge R.O.C.K.Y to a friendly sparring match, Ninja Main may attempt to learn stealth techniques from anyone who looks mysterious, and ZOO-mbie may become distracted by an animal during a serious conversation.

These interactions can help the characters feel like members of the same world rather than four unrelated additions.

---

# 7. Final Character Summary

**Bobbie Macalanger** is the confident boxer who relies on strength, footwork, and determination. His afro and distinctive sneakers make him instantly recognizable.

**R.O.C.K.Y** is the armored robotic bull who combines mechanical power with an unexpectedly compassionate personality. His oversized horns and glowing core define his silhouette.

**Ninja Main** is the enthusiastic ninja enthusiast who has not yet mastered the art of being a ninja. His awkward determination creates opportunities for both humor and meaningful character development.

**ZOO-mbie** is the former safari guide turned zombie who retains his love of wildlife despite his undead condition. His unusual appearance and chaotic abilities make him a memorable addition to the roster.

Together, the four characters provide four different gameplay experiences: aggressive melee combat, heavy defense, agile movement, and chaotic area control.

They are designed to be visually distinct, mechanically varied, and flexible enough to support future updates, additional abilities, and individual character storylines.

# 8. BANANAS

## Basic Information

- **Full Name:** BANANAS
- **Alias:** The Juicy Jumper
- **Species:** Sentient orange
- **Role:** Trickster / mobility fighter
- **Personality:** Silly, energetic, overconfident and unpredictable
- **Combat Style:** Kicks, bouncing, rolling and fruit-based chaos
- **Difficulty:** Easy to medium

## Backstory

BANANAS started life as a perfectly ordinary orange in the fruit aisle of a grocery store. A boy stole him, glued on a pair of googly eyes, drew a mouth with a marker, stuck on two little homemade legs and named him after the funniest word he knew. On a school field trip to Lab 7 the boy found an unlabelled syringe of "Experimental Growth Serum #9" in a glass case, and injected it straight into the orange. The googly eyes and marker mouth turned into a real face, BANANAS grew to human size, said his first word ("BANANAS!") and ran out of the lab before the teacher finished counting heads. He now travels in search of fame, adventure and the boy, who still owes him a better mouth.

## Appearance and Personality

BANANAS is a bright, round, dimpled orange with a stem and a wobbly green leaf, two real cartoon eyes with blue irises and eyebrows (they blink), a wide open smile, and two thin orange legs with little rounded feet (no shoes). He has no arms. His silhouette is round, orange and unmistakable: an orange, not a cinnamon bun.

He is friendly, optimistic, brave and convinced he is the greatest fruit hero alive. He announces his moves, celebrates tiny victories and refers to himself in the third person. He acts before thinking, but will throw himself into danger to help a friend.

**Catchphrase:** "NO ARMS, NO PROBLEM!"

## Abilities and Turn-Based Translation

- **Citrus Kick:** A quick, spinning 13-damage kick that interrupts a simultaneous strike.
- **Orange Bounce:** A high, squash-and-stretch hop. In the turn-based rules it dodges a single-target strike and powers up the next strike; area attacks still connect.
- **Peel Out:** A risky 12-damage roll with a 50% chance to evade a single-target strike. A failed attempt leaves BANANAS exposed next round.
- **FRUIT NUCLEAR!:** BANANAS compresses, launches like a cannonball and lands in a four-hit, 28-damage shockwave. It cannot be dodged, a block halves it, and he wobbles dizzy afterward. He yells, "BANANAS HAS ARRIVED!"

## Strengths and Weaknesses

He is mobile, difficult to predict and can disrupt opponents with a wide ultimate. He has short range, limited defense and a risky roll that can leave him vulnerable. His compact body and lack of arms are central to his visual identity; he attacks with his legs and whole body.

## Character Interactions

- **Bobbie Macalanger:** Bobbie asks how BANANAS can box without fists. BANANAS replies, "WITH MY FEET, OBVIOUSLY!" Bobbie laughs and offers to train him.
- **R.O.C.K.Y:** R.O.C.K.Y scans him as a fruit-shaped object; BANANAS dances in the scanning beam. R.O.C.K.Y classifies him as "FRIENDLY FRUIT. HIGH CHAOS POTENTIAL."
- **Ninja Main:** Ninja Main tries to teach stealth, but BANANAS keeps bouncing into walls. BANANAS calls silent bouncing a ninja technique; Ninja Main secretly writes it down.
- **ZOO-mbie:** ZOO-mbie mistakes him for a banana and tries to classify him as citrus. They become friends after BANANAS accidentally rolls downhill and rescues him. ZOO-mbie calls him "Small. Round. Loud. Possibly edible. Definitely friend."

## Animation and Future Ideas

Idle BANANAS looks around, bounces, tries to scratch his head with a foot and falls over before pretending nothing happened. His body squashes on landing and stretches in the air; his eyes widen when surprised. Victory is three higher bounces, a dramatic spin, a perfect landing, then a sideways roll out of the pose. In defeat he falls over, tries to stand, rolls onto his back and sighs, "Maybe BANANAS needs a nap."

Future ideas include fruit power-ups, cosmetic skins, bounce challenges, collectibles, a serious banana rival and a quest to find the boy who made him.

# 9. PROPHET (secret fighter)

## Basic Information

- **Name:** PROPHET (always written in capitals)
- **Title:** The Traveling Fortune Teller
- **Role:** Mysterious challenger, final opponent of every campaign
- **Unlock:** clear every other fighter's campaign, or enter the code `PROPHET` on the title screen. Until then his select-screen box shows "???" and can't be picked.

## Backstory and Appearance

PROPHET runs a tiny traveling fortune-telling wagon with a striped canopy, a crystal ball (it may be a snow globe) and a sign reading "$1 PROPHECIES". It turns up at every fight venue. His prophecies are always technically correct and completely useless ("You will be punched. Soon."), and he never gives refunds because he saw them coming. He only steps into the ring when his own prophecy says he will win. No other fighter's backstory involves him; he is the running gag that links the campaigns.

In public he wears a dark hooded cloak, so nobody has seen his face, only two glowing eyes. In the ring he throws the cloak off: he is a lean, athletic figure whose skin is a living galaxy (a Milky Way band, constellations, a small spiral galaxy and a glowing all-seeing triangle), with a blazing golden halo with rays around his whole head, one furious slanted eye with a red glow, no mouth, and a bright white aura around his outline. He floats upright with his arms at his sides and never touches the floor until he is knocked out. Every stage hides a small cameo of him: a poster, his booth, or the hooded figure in the background.

## Abilities and Turn-Based Translation

- **Nova Palm:** a starlight palm strike for 14 damage that interrupts a simultaneous strike.
- **Wormhole Warp:** he sinks into a portal to dodge a single-target strike, then pops out of a second portal beside the attacker for a 6-damage counter. Area attacks still hit.
- **Prophecy:** a beam from his eyes for 8 damage that also reads the opponent's next move, and his next strike can't be evaded (the Safari Senses tracking rule on a strike). Recharges for 2 rounds.
- **BIG BANG (ultimate):** he gathers a galaxy and sets it off for 28 damage. It can't be blocked or dodged.

He is meant to be strong: about 58% against the field in the CPU-vs-CPU simulation, above the 45–55% target for the regular roster.
